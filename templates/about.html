OUR MISSION

Technology for a cleaner tomorrow.

EcoScanPro is a college-ready, extensible waste-management education and analytics project.

01

Forecast

Scikit-learn linear regression predicts city-level kg/day values using synthetic Indian city data. Model metrics use chronological holdout.

02

Recognize

OpenCV validates and preprocesses images. A separately trained MobileNetV2 network can classify seven waste types.

03

Educate

Eco AI integrates a configurable LLM with a curated fallback knowledge base. Environmental headlines come from live RSS.
